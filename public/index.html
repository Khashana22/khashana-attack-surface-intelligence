Risk model

Asset score composition

  1. Business criticality0–45 points
  2. Environment & exposure7–15 points
  3. Authentication surface8–13 points
  4. Open finding severity × confidence0–30+ points

Scores cap at 100. Remediated, closed, and false-positive signals are excluded from active calculations.
